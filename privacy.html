Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026 | Dental Concierge

Dental Concierge ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data collected from users, dental practices, and prospective clients accessing our website and artificial intelligence scheduling services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our B2B AI integration services for dental practices:

  • Practice & Contact Details: Doctor/Contact Name, Work Email, WhatsApp/Phone Number, Practice Name, and Website URL provided through trial request forms.
  • Conversation Logs: Test chat interactions submitted through our website widget to process demonstration requests via third-party AI APIs (OpenAI / Make.com).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and basic analytics collected automatically to ensure platform security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for the following business purposes:

  • To configure and provision your 7-day trial of the Dental Concierge assistant.
  • To integrate your specified scheduling workflow (e.g., Google Calendar) with our AI system.
  • To contact you regarding your setup, onboarding, or customer support inquiries.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or practice information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Data Processors

We utilize trusted cloud infrastructure and automation providers to deliver our service, including Make.com, OpenAI, Web3Forms, and Vercel. Data handled by these processors is processed securely and solely to execute requested automation workflows.

4. Health Data & HIPAA Disclaimer

Dental Concierge operates primarily as an administrative B2B front-desk assistant for appointment inquiries and scheduling routing. We do not store medical records or protected health information (PHI) on public servers. Dental practices using our solution are responsible for ensuring their patient communication workflows comply with applicable local health regulations (including HIPAA in the United States).

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Lead data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill trial arrangements or active service agreements.
